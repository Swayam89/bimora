Why Bimora exists

Most insurance journeys ask for your budget first. That’s backwards.

A budget and a sum insured are answers. You can’t give them until someone has looked at your family, your health and what you already have. Bimora does that part first, and only asks the next question that would change its recommendation.

The usual form

  1. What’s your budget?
  2. How much sum insured do you want?
  3. Here are 40 plans, sorted by price.
  4. You pick one based on the premium.

With Bimora

  1. Who are you protecting, and what cover do you already have?
  2. Any health conditions, loans or savings that change the picture?
  3. Bimora reads the terms of the policies that could fit.
  4. You get two or three options, with the trade-offs spelled out.

Not sure of an answer? Bimora marks it unknown and helps you find out. A wrong “no” on a health question can get a claim rejected later.

How it works

From “I need insurance” to a policy you understand

One conversation, by chat or voice. You can stop and pick it up later on WhatsApp.

01

It gets to know your household

Who’s in the family, their ages, your city, the cover you already have (including the one from work, which usually ends when you change jobs), and anything that affects eligibility.

  • Family & ages
  • City
  • Existing cover
  • Health conditions
  • Income & expenses
  • Loans
  • Emergency savings
02

It reads the fine print

Bimora reads the policy wording itself, the document the insurer is bound by, for each plan that could suit you.

  • Exclusions
  • Waiting periods
  • Co-pay
  • Deductibles
  • Room rent limits
  • Underwriting
  • Premium
03

It recommends, and shows its working

A short list, not a catalogue. Each option comes with the reasons it fits and what you give up by choosing it.

04

It handles the next steps with you

Medical tests, documents, insurer questions. When you’re ready, you approve the exact policy and premium. Bimora sends you to pay, then checks with the insurer that the policy was actually issued.

The recommendation

Not the cheapest. The one that fits, and why.

Every recommendation is written for your family. If a cheaper plan exists, Bimora tells you what it leaves out so you can decide for yourself.

Recommended for your family of four

Plan A · Family floater, ₹25 lakh

Best fit
  • Covers your father’s diabetes after 1 year. Others make him wait up to 3.
  • No co-pay at his age. You won’t pay a share of every claim.
  • No room rent limit. With a cap, other hospital charges get cut in proportion too, not just the room.
  • Trade-off: a higher premium than the cheapest option. Your office cover stays as a top layer, not your main one.

Why not the cheapest plan? It charges a 20% co-pay on every claim for anyone over 60. On a ₹4 lakh hospital bill for your father, that’s ₹80,000 out of your pocket.

Illustrative example. Real recommendations use the insurer’s current terms and your actual quote.

What Bimora won’t do

Six things Bimora is built never to do.

Insurance mistakes show up years later, at the hospital. So Bimora works within a few fixed rules, and hands over to a person when it should.

  • Make up policy terms.If the policy wording doesn’t say it, Bimora won’t either.
  • Guess your premium.Prices come from the insurer, quoted for you, not from an average.
  • Treat “I don’t know” as “no”.An unanswered health question stays open until it’s answered.
  • Take payment without your approval.You see the exact policy and amount, and you say yes first.
  • Call a policy issued just because you paid.It confirms issuance with the insurer separately.
  • Handle a complicated case alone.Unclear medical history or an unusual situation goes to a Ditto advisor.

Questions

Before you start

Does Bimora cost anything?

No. Using Bimora is free. You pay the insurer’s premium only if you decide to buy a policy, and Ditto is paid through its regular insurance distribution model.

Will I still be able to speak to a person?

Yes. Bimora handles the repetitive parts: questions, policy reading, comparisons and reminders. If your case is complicated or you simply want a person, a Ditto advisor takes over with the full context, so you don’t repeat yourself.

Why does it ask about income and loans for health insurance?

Because the right cover depends on how big a hospital bill you could pay yourself. A family with strong savings and no EMIs can choose differently from one that couldn’t absorb a ₹3 lakh bill. Bimora only asks what would change its recommendation.

Does Bimora decide which policy I buy?

No. It recommends a few options and explains the trade-offs. You choose, and nothing is paid until you approve the exact policy and premium.

Can it check the policy I got from my employer?

Yes. Upload the document and Bimora will list what it covers, what it doesn’t, and the gaps that matter for your family, like parents who aren’t included or cover that ends when you change jobs.
